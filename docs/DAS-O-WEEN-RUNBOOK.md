# DAS-O-Ween Trivia — presenter runbook

This guide is for whoever runs **DAS-O-Ween Trivia**, the game-show part of the remote DAS-O-Ween
party. You run it alone, from your own laptop, while you read the Teams chat. Everyone else just
watches your shared screen. Nobody has to join anything.

The game is DASCADE's **DAS Survey Showdown** in its **Presenter Showcase** format, with the
**DAS-O-Ween 2026** preset loaded. Site: <https://dascade.onrender.com>.

---

## The one rule: share the stage, never the console

You see two windows:

| Window | What it shows | Share it? |
|---|---|---|
| **Presenter console** (your browser tab) | Every answer, the controls, the clock | **Never.** Only you see the answers here. |
| **Stage window** (a pop-up the console opens) | The public show: the board, the host, the effects | **Yes.** In Teams, share *this window*. |

The answers are never sent to the stage window or to anyone's phone until you flip them. Sharing the
wrong window is the only way they could leak, so always check what Teams shows before you start.

---

## Before the day: rehearse once (20 minutes)

1. Open <https://dascade.onrender.com/play/showdown> in **Chrome or Edge** on the laptop you'll present
   from. Type your name and press **Create game**. You're the presenter.
2. In **Settings → Event presets**, press **Load** next to **DAS-O-Ween rehearsal**. It loads the show
   with two *practice* boards (candy and costumes), so you can try every button without giving the real answers away. The console and
   the stage say **REHEARSAL** while those boards are loaded.
3. Press **Open stage window**. If your browser blocks it, allow pop-ups for the site and press it again.
4. Start a Teams meeting with a colleague (or just yourself on a second device). Share the stage
   window (see "Sharing in Teams" below). Check the picture *and the sound* on the other end.
5. Press **Start game** and run a whole practice round:
   - the opening and the first category card;
   - show the question;
   - reveal a few answers, including **#5**;
   - press **Wrong** several times;
   - pause the clock and add a minute;
   - press **Reveal remaining**;
   - go to the next round;
   - finish the show;
   - put up the **Prize time** card.
6. When you're done, press **Play again**. Then load the **DAS-O-Ween 2026** preset, press
   **Edit your rounds** and read through the real boards (see "Changing the questions").

> Tip: you can also rehearse in a second room. A room is just a code. Create one for practice and a
> fresh one on the day.

## On the day

### 15 minutes before (while people arrive)

1. Open <https://dascade.onrender.com/play/showdown> and press **Create game**. The site may take up
   to a minute to wake up the first time, so do this early.
2. In **Settings → Event presets**, press **Load** next to **DAS-O-Ween 2026**. That sets everything up:
   - the title "DAS-O-Ween Trivia" and the Halloween Night look;
   - the two Top-10 boards, each with a 10-minute clock;
   - **#5** as the special answer.
3. Press **Open stage window**. Drag it to your second monitor and press its **Full screen** button. On
   a single screen, put the stage window and the console side by side.
4. Under **Stage cards** on your setup card, press **Welcome**. The stage then shows "Remoties DAS-O-Ween Happy Hour" while
   people arrive and the backgrounds are judged.
5. Press **Test sound** (on your setup card now, and in the console's top bar during the show). You
   should hear a ding. If it says *Click to enable sound*, click anywhere on the page once.

### Sharing in Teams

1. In the Teams meeting, press **Share**.
2. Turn on **Include sound** (on a Mac, Teams may ask you to install its audio driver once; allow it).
3. Choose **Window** and pick the window called **"DAS-O-Ween Trivia — Stage"**. If you use a second
   monitor that shows only the stage, sharing that **screen** is fine too.
4. Check the preview in Teams: you should see the stage (the Welcome card), **not** the console.

Keep the stage window open: don't minimise it, or Teams freezes on its last picture. If you only
have one screen, it's fine for the console to cover part of the stage window, because Teams still
captures the whole stage window.

### Event cue sheet (60 minutes)

| Time | What's happening | What you do in DASCADE |
|---|---|---|
| 0–15 | Introductions, judging Halloween backgrounds | Leave the **Welcome** card up (or don't share yet) |
| 15–20 | "Remoties DAS-O-Ween Happy Hour" opening, then the old Halloween cartoon | **Welcome** card while you introduce it. For the cartoon, stop sharing the stage and share the video, then share the stage again afterwards. |
| 20 | The game starts | Turn the card off and press **Start game**. The opening plays with a fanfare and the host floats in. |
| 20–32 | Round 1: Top 10 Halloween Songs | Start round 1, then show the question (the 10-minute clock starts). Reveal what people name, press **Wrong** for misses. When the clock runs out: **Reveal remaining**. |
| 32–33 | Intermission | **Next round**: the category card for round 2 appears with its sting |
| 33–45 | Round 2: Top 10 Halloween Movies | The same again. Then **Finish the show**: the closing card, the host's farewell and applause. |
| 45–50 | Second cartoon / comedy clip | Put up **Be right back**, or share the clip instead |
| 50–60 | Prize winners | Put up **Prize time**, and **Thanks for playing** at the end |

## Running a round

The console always shows a **Next step** box that says what to do now. The main controls:

| What | Button | Key |
|---|---|---|
| Start a round / show the question | **Start round 1**, then **Show the question** (this starts the clock) | `Q` |
| Someone named an answer on the board | Click that answer on your **answer sheet** (#1–#10) | `1`–`9`, `0` = #10 |
| Someone guessed wrong | **WRONG** (press it as often as you like; it's never a strike) | `X` |
| Celebrate again (any time) | **Celebrate!** | `C` |
| Pause / resume the clock | **Pause clock** / **Start clock**; **+1 min** / **−1 min** to change it | `P` |
| Time's up: show what's left | **Reveal remaining**. Press **Stop revealing** to pause it; press **Reveal remaining** again to carry on. | `R` (Esc stops) |
| Next round / finish | **Next round** / **Finish the show** | `N` |
| Oops | **Undo** (undoes one step at a time, including restarts) | `U` |

What happens:

- **Correct answer:** a ding and a metallic clang, and the tile flips. The host reacts.
- **#5, the special answer:** the big DAS-O-Ween celebration with lights, banner, confetti, a fanfare
  and the host going wild. It also plays if #5 comes up during **Reveal remaining** (with a drumroll
  first).
- **Wrong:** a buzzer and a giant circled ✕ that disappears after a moment. Nothing is counted.
- **The clock** never reveals anything by itself. At 0:00 the console tells you to wrap up, and the
  stage shows "Final guesses!". Take one or two more guesses, then press **Reveal remaining**.
- **Reveal remaining** flips the answers nobody found, from #10 up to #1, skipping the ones already
  showing. Answers found by the crowd stay bright; the ones revealed at the end look different, so
  everyone can see what they got.

If something goes wrong:

- **Undo** reverses the last step.
- Under **Fix a mistake**, **Restart this round** puts the round back to its category card with every answer hidden.
- **Restart the whole show** goes back to the opening.

All three ask for confirmation, and you can undo them too.

## Changing the questions

**Settings → Edit your rounds.** For each round you can change:

- the category ("Halloween Songs"), the question ("Name the Top 10 Halloween Songs.") and the
  attribution, which the stage shows as **"According to …"**;
- the answers and their order. The rounds are a *ranked list*: no points, the order you see is the
  order on the board, and the ▲ ▼ buttons move answers;
- each answer's small line (artist or year), shown under the answer once it's revealed;
- the other spellings accepted from phones;
- which answer is the **special** one (#5 by default).

The preset lists are **DAS-O-Ween Host Picks**, a curated list, *not* a survey. Keep that
attribution, or put in a real source if you use a real ranking. Don't name a poll that didn't
happen.

Press **Save to the show**. To keep your edits for another day (or another room), open **Save, load
and share**, type a name and press **Save on this device**. Later, in a new room, **Load a saved show**
brings the rounds *and* the settings back.

## Optional: phones

Anyone who wants to can open **dascade.onrender.com/r/CODE** (the code is on the Welcome card and in
the corner of the stage).

- On their phone they get a mini board and a cheer pad. Their cheers float up on the stage, and a
  big "Boo!" makes the host jump.
- Phones are silent by default, so the room doesn't echo your stage sound.
- Private guesses on phones are **off** for DAS-O-Ween, because people guess in the Teams chat. You
  can switch them on in Settings ("Phones can lock in private guesses").

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Fix |
|---|---|
| The stage window doesn't open | Allow pop-ups for dascade.onrender.com and press **Stage window** again. Or open the **Big-screen link** on another computer and share that. |
| No sound in Teams | Re-share with **Include sound** on. Make sure the console says sound is on (top bar) and press **Test sound**. Unmute the browser tab. |
| The console says "Click to enable sound" | Click anywhere in the console once. Browsers need one click before they play sound. |
| You closed the stage window | Press **Stage window** in the console again, then re-pick the window in Teams. |
| You reloaded or closed the console | Open the room link again in the same browser. You're the presenter again automatically, and the show is exactly where it was. |
| You need to run it from another laptop | **Presenter tools → Copy presenter link** (keep it private). Open it on the other laptop. |
| The whole site went down | Create a new room, load **DAS-O-Ween 2026** (or your saved show), start, and use **Next round** to jump to where you were. |

Shortcut keys don't work while you're typing in a text box. Click the console background first.
